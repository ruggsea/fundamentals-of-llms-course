# Stickers

Public-domain engraving cutouts for the slides. Every PNG is a transparent cutout, recoloured to ink `rgb(38,34,30)`, tightly cropped, 800 to 1200 px on the long side, under 400 KB. `_sheet.png` shows them all on the cream background (`#f3ecd9`).

Cutting: greyscale, paper level taken from the image, darkness mapped to alpha with a curve (paper goes fully transparent, ink stays solid), specks removed, cropped to the bounding box with a 14 px margin. Sources under 1000 px were upscaled (Lanczos) before thresholding: `gear`, `quill`, `laurel`, `telegraph-key`.

Style note: the Pearson Scott Foresman files (wrench, scales, dice, bell) are bolder 20th-century textbook line art rather than period engravings. They sit fine next to the rest at slide size, but they are the first to replace if a period cut turns up.

Not found with a clean licence: a magnifying glass. The optional items (hands holding a pen, chalkboard or ledger) were also skipped.
